## Defect and reproduction

Finding F13 (unresolved hypothesis). Historical observation (memory 2026-09-05, battery log): 55 of 354 completed jobs finished with `best_metric = 0.0000` and `Tie-break: 100.00%` (every catalogue item at an identical score); VBPR 0/96, VNPR native 3/112 (2.7 %), VNPR × hybrid 52/146 (35.6 %); deterministic per cell; worst on scale-distorting fusions (sigmoid_gated 58 %, weighted_mean_w0.3 56 %, prod 54 %, mean 50 %), mildest on sum (8 %). Historical logs carry no branch/score/gradient probes, so nothing can be re-validated from them — hence this task instruments first.

What the probes can now distinguish (all measured on the same fixed probe, at init / fixed steps / every validation):

## Implementation

Files: `src/utils/diagnostics.py` (new), `src/utils/training.py` (hook), `src/recommenders/vnpr.py` (pre-activation accessor), `configs/default.yaml` (`diagnostics:` block, off), `scripts/vnpr_collapse_diagnostic.py` (new).

- Probe: seeded (`probe_seed`) users with ≥ 1 training interaction, one training positive and one non-training negative per user, a sorted item subset, fixed item pairs. Defaults 64 users / 256 items / 64 pairs; nothing scales with the catalogue.
- Records (`init`, `step` at `steps`, default `[1, 10, 100, 1000]`): feature norm quantiles per source (3-D stack → per slice; ragged → split by `source_dims`) and fused; `train_branches` (train mode, dropout on) and `eval_branches` (eval mode, `(u, i, i)`) min/median/max/mean/active fraction; score min/max/mean/std/unique fraction/constant-user fraction/top-tie fraction/all-tied-user fraction/pairwise zero fraction and |Δ| quantiles; `loss.{bpr,l2,total,finite}` from one throw-away backward on the probe triple; per-parameter gradient norms and the total; per-parameter `norm`, `tracked_norm` (probe rows of user/item tables, whole small tensors), `delta_from_prev`, `delta_from_init`, `zero_row_fraction`; `optimizer.{steps_attempted,steps_applied,steps_skipped,scaler_enabled,scaler_scale}` (Adam step counter, so AMP-skipped steps show as skipped).
- Validation records: `metric_value`, `metric_present`, `metric_finite`, `zero_metric`, `tie_frequency` (probe top-tie fraction), `checkpoint_exists` (trial-best file), `has_valid_observation`, all metrics, and the probe `scores` block.
- Isolation: every measurement runs inside `torch.random.fork_rng` (CPU; CUDA device added when training on it), under `no_grad` except the one backward, whose gradients are cleared with `zero_grad(set_to_none=True)`; the module's train/eval mode is restored; optimizer state is only read. `test_off_by_default_writes_nothing_and_leaves_training_unchanged` trains VNPR twice (off / on) and asserts identical selection metric and bit-identical final weights; `test_measurement_does_not_disturb_rng_parameters_or_gradients` asserts the RNG state, parameters and (absent) gradients after a record with dropout 0.5.
- Output: `<output_dir>/<run_id>.json`, written once at the end of `train_single_run` (in its `finally`, so a failed trial still leaves its probes), via `atomic_write`.
- Driver: `scripts/vnpr_collapse_diagnostic.py` (docstring documents conditions and usage). Same split, sampler, budget, init policy and hyperparameters across conditions; only the visual input differs. Seeds ≥ 3 by default (warns below); learning rates 1e-3 and 1e-2 (the two aggravator levels of the battery memory). Writes per-run JSON, `summary.json` (reconciled expected/completed/zero/failed per condition) and `report.md` with the SPEC's decision table filled by simple rules from the probes (`decide()`); "cause still unresolved" is printed unless the user runs it on real data.

Scientific invariants: none touched. No change to VNPR's ReLU, loss, regulariser, sampler, candidate set, tie policy or selection cadence. The only in-loop additions are a step counter and guarded calls that are skipped when diagnostics are off.

### Reading of the synthetic run

- On synthetic finite inputs of the same scale ratio as the real extractors (‖a‖ ≈ 10, ‖b‖ ≈ 30, fused learned ‖f‖ ≈ 0.7 after normalisation), VNPR does **not** collapse under any condition: no dead branch at init (active fraction 0.31–0.66 across 42 runs), gradients finite and non-zero, every optimizer step applied (20/20, no AMP on CPU), parameters move, scores remain non-constant, no zero metric, a winner checkpoint exists at every validation.
- The scale-only controls do not reproduce a collapse either: `native_a` → `native_a_unit` (same directions, norm 9.6 → 1) lowers the mean best ndcg@10 (0.073 → 0.044) but raises the final active fraction; `learned_mean_nonorm` → `learned_mean` (‖f‖ 15 → 0.72) behaves the same way. So on this data the smaller visual term costs metric, not viability — consistent with "scale correlates with metric level" but not with "scale alone kills the ReLU".
- Therefore none of the decision table's rows is observed here, and the synthetic run cannot support or reject any hypothesis about amazon_women / tradesy. The probes are validated (the forced-dead-model test shows exactly what a collapse looks like in every field), the driver is ready, and the discriminating experiment is the real-data run.
