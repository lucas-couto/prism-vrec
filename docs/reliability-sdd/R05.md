## Defect and reproduction

Confirmed in the current checkout:

1. `src/steps/statistical.py` wrote `{dataset}_{kind}.csv` regardless of `condition`; `--condition frozen` then `finetuned` (or `all`) overwrote the previous comparison silently.
2. No reconciliation of tested cells against the evaluate step's completion record; a cell recorded done but absent from the table, or evaluated on fewer users, went unreported (and, before R04, was inner-joined).
3. `src/reporting/aggregate_seeds.py` counted rows (`count`/`size`) as `n_seeds`: a duplicated seed source reported 4 seeds for 2; conflicting rows for one seed were averaged.
4. `src/folds/aggregate.py` located partials by filename only: a partial with another `d` / `eval_protocol_version` / `n_items`, a torn partial (`fold.n_users` != rows) or two partials with one fold seed were concatenated.

Evidence: `tests/test_statistical_step_integrity.py` (12 of 15 failed before), `tests/test_fold_aggregate.py::TestPartialIdentity` (5 failed before); all pass after.
